What each can borrow from the other

Integris, from First Focus

  • Publish prices. First Focus shows per-user prices and add-on rates; Integris shows none. This is the largest gap on the scorecard (1 vs 7).
  • Publish an AI info page. First Focus has one; the AI Page example on the Integris site is a ready draft.
  • Put service levels in writing. Response times, a defined SLA and uptime figures, the way First Focus does on IT Support and CORE.
  • Add Service markup. First Focus's structured data already describes services and offers.

First Focus, from Integris

  • A fuller Organization record. Integris's structured data includes address, hours, employees and social profiles; First Focus's has only a name and logo.
  • Fix quickly once flagged. Integris fixed its canonical, its nofollow tag and most of its typos within hours of the first scan.
  • One page per audience, not per city. Integris's single Locations page avoids nine templated city pages that add little for AI.

Both, together

  • State the relationship. Neither site says First Focus is now part of Integris in a way AI can read: First Focus still says "100% Australian-owned," and Integris mentions the acquisition only in press releases.
  • Use one Shamrock story. The same client appears as an 80% productivity gain on both sites and as four days to four hours with a 2.8x ROI on Integris's CORE page.
  • Give agents a way in. Both lose AI buying agents at the contact step. The HubSpot fix plan on each site's seller cards page applies to both.

How the scores were set

Every criterion counts equally, 10 points each, so no single area dominates the total. Scores are judgments based on the page reports from October 29, 2026, and are meant to be challenged and re-scored as fixes land. Integris's score reflects its 3:04 pm rescan.
